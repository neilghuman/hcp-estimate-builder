👋 Start here

Chat Foundry sends bulk messages through Chatwoot — but only when you push a message through three separate, confirmed steps. Nothing is ever sent by accident. Work top to bottom:

  1. Set up once — open Setup & connection, check the connection, and load inboxes & tags. (Skip this after the first time.)
  2. Pick who — in Audience, choose a status/tags and click Refresh Audience. Read-only preview; nothing sends.
  3. Write it — in Compose, type your message (use {{first_name}} style fields), Preview against samples, and optionally have the AI Suggest a rewrite.
  4. Send safely — in Campaign, create a campaign, build the recipient list, send one test, then do the gated bulk send (with pause/resume/cancel + live progress).
  5. Review — Campaign history shows every send with recipient drill-down, an audit log, and CSV export.
